Sheep Tracker Overview
	My project will kepp track of a herd of sheep! Each sheep is a unique key in the map, and each sheep has an array of 3 lists of dates. The lists represent milking, eating, and shearing. We will simulate one day on the farm at a time, and look at methods of keeping sheep fed, sheared, and milked. Not every sheep may need to be sheared every day, nor will every sheep be especially hungry. However, other sheep maybe be extra hungry and take an extra helping of food, depreiving another sheep! The program will let the user know when a particular sheep has gone a long time without being fed, milked, or sheared, or when an excess of food has been provided to the sheep. We can rerun the simulation with different algorithms that determine how much food to give the sheep and when to shear them, and see how much under/over shearing, under/over milking, and under/over feeding happens each cycle.
